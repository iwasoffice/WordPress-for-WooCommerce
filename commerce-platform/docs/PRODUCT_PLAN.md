# Product plan

## Phase 1: Beauty & Hair

Focus on non-liquid, low-complexity accessories:

- Extra-long adjustable satin bonnet.
- Double-layer satin bonnet.
- Satin pillowcase.
- Satin scrunchie multipack.
- Wig grip band.
- Wig storage/travel bag.
- Large protective-style shower cap.
- Detangling brush.
- Sectioning clips.
- Edge brush/comb set.

Avoid unverified hair-growth claims, ingestibles, creams, oils and powered styling devices at launch.

## Phase 2: Tech & Gadgets

Start with lower-risk, non-powered products:

- Laptop stands.
- Laptop/tablet sleeves.
- Desk mats.
- Cable organisers.
- Phone/tablet stands.
- Cleaning kits.
- Travel cable pouches.

Actual phones, computers, batteries, power banks and chargers should only be added after supplier quality, warranty, fraud and returns processes have been proven.
